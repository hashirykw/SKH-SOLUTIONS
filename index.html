About SKH Solutions

One roof. 35 specialists. Every subject.

SKH stands for Sir Khurram Hussain — an O Level Islamiyat teacher who has taught at some of Karachi's leading schools since 2008. SKH Solutions grew from his classes into a complete O & A Level institute, bringing together experienced teachers for sciences, commerce and humanities across six campuses in Karachi.

Meet the Founder

The teacher behind SKH.

Sir Khurram Hussain began teaching professionally in 2008. He holds a BA (Hons) in Islamiyat & Computer Science from Sheikh Zayed Islamic University and a Master's in Islamic Studies from the University of Karachi, and has taught at Beaconhouse School System, Karachi Grammar School, Shahwilayat Public School and Nakhla Islamic School.

        Can private candidates register through you?

        Yes. We help private candidates with CAIE registration and guide them through the whole process.
